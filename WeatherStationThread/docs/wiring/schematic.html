Check before powering

  • The moisture sensor's power wire color isn't in the readme. It goes to 3V3, not to the vane's switched power.
  • Rain gets a new 1M pull-down; the old 10K drew 330 µA whenever the bucket rested closed.
  • The anemometer pull-up is new. The old code used the chip's internal one.
  • PlusRoc no-load output must measure 6.0 V or less before it goes near VCC.
