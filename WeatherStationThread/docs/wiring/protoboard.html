Weather station protoboard

Placement for the harness landing board. The FireBeetle stays off the board and connects with Dupont wires to its header, like the old build.

  • Colored dot: a harness wire lands here, in its wire color. A white dot with a colored ring is a striped wire (orange/white, blue/white).
  • Purple square: a Dupont wire to the FireBeetle header pin named in the table.
  • Tan blocks are resistors, stood up between the hole and the rail. Black bars are jumpers. Dark blocks are the FETs on SOT-23-to-DIP adapters.

Before soldering

Beep out the board with a meter first. The copper ring around the edge joins some rail rows; this plan leaves both outer rails and the ring empty, so check that neither inner rail connects to the ring.

  • Pull-ups sit in the top half (3V3 rail is the inner top row) and pull-downs in the bottom half (GND rail is the inner bottom row), so no resistor reaches across a rail.
  • The FETs are SOT-23 parts: solder each onto a SOT-23-to-DIP adapter first. Check the adapter's pin 1 marking; both parts are 1 = gate, 2 = source, 3 = drain.
  • Each top/bottom column is one 5-hole strip, so the harness wire, resistor and FireBeetle wire in a column are already joined.
  • Power the board from the bench first and check: 3V3 on the top rail, GPIO7 column at 3.3 V (pull-up), f27 at 5 V when the PlusRoc is on.
